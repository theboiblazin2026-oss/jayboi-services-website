Why Is This Required?

📅

Every 2 Years

The "Biennial" update is mandatory for all carriers with a DOT number, regardless of activity status.

🚫

Risk of Deactivation

Failure to update your MCS-150 can result in your DOT number being deactivated, stopping your trucks cold.

💰

Heavy Fines

Operating with outdated information or a deactivated DOT number can lead to penalties of $1,000 per day.

⚡

Fast Filing

We submit your update directly to the FMCSA system. You get confirmation of the update same-day.

When Is Your Update Due?

It depends on the last two digits of your USDOT Number.

Second to Last Digit

Determines the Year

  • Odd Number = Odd Years (2025, 2027...)
  • Even Number = Even Years (2026, 2028...)

Last Digit

Determines the Month

  • 1 = January
  • 2 = February
  • 3 = March
  • 4 = April
  • 5 = May
  • 6 = June
  • 7 = July
  • 8 = August
  • 9 = September
  • 0 = October
