2026 Trucking Compliance Calendar

Published: January 19, 2026

Never miss a deadline! Here are all the important 2026 compliance dates every motor carrier needs to know.

Q1 (January - March)

  • January 31 - IFTA Q4 2025 filing deadline
  • January 31 - IRP renewal deadline (varies by state)
  • March 1 - UCR renewal deadline

Q2 (April - June)

  • April 30 - IFTA Q1 2026 filing deadline
  • June 30 - MCS-150 biennial update (odd-numbered USDOT)

Q3 (July - September)

  • July 31 - IFTA Q2 2026 filing deadline
  • August 31 - Form 2290 (HVUT) due for July vehicles

Q4 (October - December)

  • October 31 - IFTA Q3 2026 filing deadline
  • December 31 - MCS-150 biennial update (even-numbered USDOT)
  • December 31 - UCR registration opens for 2027

Ongoing Requirements

  • ✅ Annual Clearinghouse queries for all CDL drivers
  • ✅ Annual MVR reviews
  • ✅ Medical certificate renewals (every 1-2 years)
  • ✅ Drug & alcohol random testing (throughout year)
