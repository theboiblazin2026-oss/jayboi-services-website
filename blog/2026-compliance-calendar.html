COMPLIANCE CALENDAR

2026 Trucking Compliance Calendar

Published: January 19, 2026

Stay on top of every important compliance deadline in 2026. Missing a deadline can result in fines, penalties, or even being shut down.

📅 Quarterly Deadlines

IFTA Quarterly Filing

Q1 2026 Due: April 30, 2026
Q2 2026 Due: July 31, 2026
Q3 2026 Due: October 31, 2026
Q4 2026 Due: January 31, 2027

📋 Annual Deadlines

  • Form 2290 (HVUT) - Due: August 31, 2026
  • UCR Registration - Due: December 31, 2026
  • IRP Renewal - Varies by base state (check your cab card)
  • IFTA Decal Renewal - December 31, 2026

🔄 Biennial Requirements

  • MCS-150 Update - Every 2 years, based on your USDOT# ending
  • DOT Physical - Every 2 years for drivers (some require annually)

⚡ Ongoing Requirements

  • Annual Clearinghouse queries for all drivers
  • Annual MVR checks for all drivers
  • Random drug testing (minimum 25% of drivers)
  • Annual review of driving records
