Driver Qualification Files: Complete Checklist

Published: January 19, 2026

Motor carriers are required to maintain Driver Qualification (DQ) files for each driver. These files prove that your drivers meet FMCSA requirements.

Required Documents

  • ✅ Copy of CDL (front and back)
  • ✅ Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) - annual
  • ✅ Road test certificate or equivalent
  • ✅ Medical examiner's certificate
  • ✅ Previous employer verification (past 3 years)
  • ✅ Annual review of driving record
  • ✅ FMCSA Clearinghouse query results

Retention Requirements

Most DQ file documents must be retained for 3 years after employment ends. Drug and alcohol records have specific retention periods ranging from 1-5 years.

Common Audit Violations

  • ❌ Missing or expired medical certificates
  • ❌ No annual MVR on file
  • ❌ Incomplete employment verification
  • ❌ Missing Clearinghouse queries
