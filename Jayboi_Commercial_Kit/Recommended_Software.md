# Recommended Video Editors for Mac

Since you are looking for the **best free option with the most features**, here are the top 3 recommendations for assembling your Jayboi commercial.

## 1. DaVinci Resolve (The "Hollywood" Choice) 🏆

**Verdict:** Best Overall for "Most Options" (completely free).

* **Cost:** **FREE** (Standard version).
* **Pros:** It is a professional Hollywood-grade editor. It has the best color grading in the world, professional audio mixing (Fairlight), and visual effects (Fusion) all built-in. No watermarks.
* **Cons:** Higher learning curve. It looks like a cockpit.
* **Best For:** If you want your video to look like a high-end TV commercial.
* **Download:** [Blackmagic Design Website](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)

## 2. CapCut Desktop (The "Social Media" Choice) ⚡

**Verdict:** Best for Speed & Effects.

* **Cost:** **FREE** (with optional Paid Pro features).
* **Pros:** Extremely easy to use. Built-in auto-captions, trendy transitions, and effects. Very fast workflow for social media ads.
* **Cons:** Some of the best features are locked behind a monthly subscription.
* **Best For:** If you want to get this done *fast* and ready for Instagram/TikTok.
* **Download:** Mac App Store.

## 3. iMovie (The "Already There" Choice) 🍎

**Verdict:** Best for Simplicity.

* **Cost:** **FREE** (Built-in).
* **Pros:** You already have it. Zero cost, zero download. Very simple "drag and drop".
* **Cons:** Very limited. Hard to do fancy text overlays or the "crop & zoom" 4-panel split screen technique we discussed.
* **Best For:** If you just want to put clips in order and don't care about fancy effects.

## My Recommendation for YOU

**Go with DaVinci Resolve** if you are willing to spend an hour watching a tutorial. It fits your request for "Free" + "Most Options" perfectly.

**Go with CapCut** if you want it done *tonight*.
