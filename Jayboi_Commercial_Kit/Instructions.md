# Jayboi Services Commercial Video Kit

This kit contains the assets and script needed to produce a professional commercial for Jayboi Services.

## Contents

1. **Script_And_Storyboard.md**: The full 45-second script with visual directions and voiceover lines.
2. **assets/**: Folder containing initial visual assets.
    * `logo.png`: The transparent company logo.
    * `reference_ad.mp4`: A screen-captured website walkthrough (useful for b-roll).
    * `scene_1_stressed_driver.jpg`: Sunset driver image.
    * `scene_2_paperwork.png`: Urgent paperwork image.
    * `scene_3_dispatcher.png`: Professional dispatcher image.
    * `scene_4_services_montage.jpg`: Services collage image.
    * `scene_5_happy_trucker.png`: Success driver image.
    * `scene_extra_reality_montage.jpg`: Bonus 4-panel split screen image.

## How to Edit This Video

Since you want to edit this locally to get it to spec:

1. **Import Assets**: Open your video editor (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut, or CapCut) and import the files in the `assets` directory.
2. **Record Voiceover**: Record the lines from the `Script_And_Storyboard.md` file. You can do this yourself or use an AI voiceover tool (like ElevenLabs).
3. **Source Footage**: The script calls for "real world" characters. You can use the provided AI images or replace them with stock footage.
4. **Assemble**:
    * Lay down the voiceover audio track first.
    * Add background music.
    * Place the visuals to match the script cues.
    * Overlay the text titles (e.g., "COMPLIANCE MATTERS") as specified in the script.

    > **💡 Advanced Editing Tip (Using the 4-panel image):**
    > You have a 4-panel montage image (`scene_extra_reality_montage.jpg`). You can use this to create **4 separate shots**!
    > Simply place the image on your timeline and **Scale/Zoom in to 200%**, then **Position** the frame to show only one quadrant (e.g., just the top-left driver scene).
    > * **Use Top-Left (Night Driver)** in Scene 1 to show the day turning into a long night.
    > * **Use Top-Right (Violation Notice)** in Scene 2 to heighten the stakes.
    > * **Use Bottom-Left (Permits)** and **Bottom-Right (Dispatch Team)** in Scene 4 to illustrate specific services.

5. **Export**: Export as MP4/H.264 for social media.

## Note on Assets

The kit includes high-quality AI-generated reference images. Feel free to use them directly or use them as a "shot list" to film your own team for maximum authenticity.
