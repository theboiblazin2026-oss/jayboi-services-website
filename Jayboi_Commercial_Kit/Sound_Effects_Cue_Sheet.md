# Sound Effects (SFX) Cue Sheet

Professional audio makes the difference between a "slideshow" and a "commercial". Use this list to find and place sounds in your editor.

## 0:00 - 0:08 (Scene 1: Stressed Driver)

* **Ambience:** Low, steady diesel engine idle (rumbling bass).
* **SFX:** Faint "static" or CB radio chatter in the background (adds realism).
* **Action:** Loud "Sigh" or exhaling breath sound when the video starts.

## 0:08 - 0:15 (Scene 2: The Stakes)

* **Transition:** A "Whoosh" or "Slam" sound when the scene cuts.
* **SFX:** Loud, heavy **"Ink Stamp Thud"** sound right when the "URGENT" picture appears. This should be louder than the music for impact.
* **Ambience:** Ticking clock sound (subtle, building tension).

## 0:15 - 0:25 (Scene 3: The Solution)

* **Transition:** "Digital Glitch" or "Clean Swoosh" sound.
* **Ambience:** Quiet office room tone (no engine noise).
* **SFX:** Typing keyboard sounds (light, fast clicking).
* **Music Shift:** The music should "open up" here – maybe add a cymbal swell.

## 0:25 - 0:35 (Scene 4: Services)

* **SFX:** "Pop" or "Ding" sounds for each new slide/pan:
    1. Ifta Sticker -> "Pop"
    2. Map -> "High Tech Beep"
    3. Safety -> "Checkmark/Writing" sound.

## 0:35 - 0:40 (Scene 5: Happy Driver)

* **Ambience:** Highway wind noise (whooshing by).
* **SFX:** Truck horn honk (distant, triumphant) at the end of the scene.

## 0:40 - 0:45 (Scene 6: CTA)

* **SFX:** "Whoosh" transition to the logo.
* **SFX:** "Ding!" content when the phone number appears.
