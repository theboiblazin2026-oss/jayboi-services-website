# Jayboi Services - "Keep On Trucking" Commercial Script

**Duration:** 45 Seconds
**Music:** Upbeat, driving classic rock or energetic corporate background track.

## SCENE 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:08)

**Visual:** A truck driver (mid 40s, flannel shirt, cap) sitting in his cab, looking stressed. He's holding a crumpled piece of paper, rubbing his forehead. Outside the window, it's sunset.
**Audio (Voiceover):** "You didn't get into trucking to push paper. You got in this to drive."
**Text Overlay:** BURIED IN PAPERWORK?

## SCENE 2: The Stakes (0:08 - 0:15)

**Visual:** Quick montage of confusing tax forms (2290), DOT numbers, and compliance checklists piling up on a desk. A red "OVERDUE" stamp slams down.
**Audio (Voiceover):** "But one missed deadline or filing error can park your rig faster than an empty tank."
**Text Overlay:** COMPLIANCE MATTERS.

## SCENE 3: The Solution (0:15 - 0:25)

**Visual:** Transition to a clean, modern dashboard interface (Jayboi Branding). A professional dispatcher (female, smiling, headset) is calm and typing on a computer.
**Audio (Voiceover):** "Enter Jayboi Services. We handle the red tape, so you can handle the road."
**Text Overlay:** WE HANDLE THE RED TAPE.

## SCENE 4: The Services (0:25 - 0:35)

**Visual:** Split screen or sliding panels showing:

1. **Compliance:** IFTA Stickers / Authority Docs.

3. **Safety:** A driver doing a thumbs up during a pre-trip inspection.
**Audio (Voiceover):** "From Authority Setup and 2290 filings to premium Dispatching and Compliance management. We've got your back office covered."

## SCENE 5: The Success (0:35 - 0:40)

**Visual:** The original truck driver, now smiling, rolling down the highway on a clear sunny day. He waves out the window.
**Audio (Voiceover):** "Drive with confidence. Drive with Jayboi."
**Text Overlay:** DRIVE WITH CONFIDENCE.

**Visual:** Jayboi Services Logo on a black/navy background. Phone number and website animated in.
