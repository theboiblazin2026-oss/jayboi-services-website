FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

What is the FMCSA Drug and Alcohol Clearinghouse?

The FMCSA Clearinghouse is a secure online database that gives employers and agencies real-time information about CDL driver drug and alcohol program violations. It was created to improve safety by preventing drivers with unresolved violations from operating commercial vehicles.

Are annual Clearinghouse queries mandatory?

Yes. Employers must conduct a limited query at least once every 12 months for every CDL driver currently employed to check for unresolved drug or alcohol violations.

What is the new CDL Downgrade rule?

Effective November 18, 2024, if a driver's Clearinghouse status is 'Prohibited' due to a drug or alcohol violation, State Driver Licensing Agencies (SDLAs) are required to immediately downgrade their CDL, revoking their commercial driving privileges until the Return-to-Duty process is complete.

What is a Pre-Employment Query?

Before hiring any CDL driver, carriers are legally required to conduct a 'Full Query' in the Clearinghouse to verify that the prospective employee is not prohibited from performing safety-sensitive functions.
