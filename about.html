We are more than just a service provider. We are your partner in the trucking industry.

Founded With A Purpose

Jayboi Services, LLC was founded by Calvin Manning with a single mission: to simplify the complex world of DOT compliance for owner-operators and fleet managers.

With over 15 years of logistics experience, we understand the headaches that come with audits, paperwork, and regulations. We saw too many good drivers getting fined or shut down simply because they didn't have the backend support they needed.

We created Jayboi Services to be that support system. A "United Family" where we treat every client's business with the same care and dedication as our own.
