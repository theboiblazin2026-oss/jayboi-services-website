FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Dispatch Questions

What does a truck dispatcher actually do for owner-operators?

A truck dispatcher finds high-paying freight, negotiates rates with brokers on your behalf, handles all the setup packets and rate confirmations, plans your routes, and manages the back-office communication so you can focus entirely on driving safely.

How much does Jayboi Services charge for dispatching?

We charge a competitive 8% gross weekly fee. This percentage structure ensures our goals are aligned with yours—we only make more money when we negotiate higher-paying loads for your trucks.

Do you offer forced dispatch?

No. At Jayboi Services, we operate with a strict 'No Forced Dispatch' policy. You are the boss. We present the best load options, but you retain full decision-making authority over where you drive and what freight you haul.

Do I need my own active authority to use your dispatch service?

Yes. We work exclusively with carriers and owner-operators who possess their own active Motor Carrier (MC) Authority.
