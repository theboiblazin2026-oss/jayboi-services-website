Jayboi Services

1. MC/DOT Authority Setup

Service Fee: $900.00 + FMCSA Fees ($300 filing fee)
Timeline: 7-21 business days
Objective: Register new motor carrier authority with FMCSA
Filing Portal: https://li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov/LIVIEW/pkg_uis_landing.prc_login

Required Documentation from Customer

Step-by-Step Process

Phase 1: Initial Setup (Day 1-2)

  1. Create customer folder in filing system:
    • Format: [Customer Name] - MC/DOT Setup - [Date]
    • Example: ABC Trucking LLC - MC/DOT Setup - 2026-01-10
    • Create subfolders: Applications, Correspondence, Insurance, Completed
  2. Send welcome email with document checklist
    • Use email template from Templates folder
    • Attach document checklist PDF
    • Set expected timeline: 7-21 business days
    • Include your direct contact information
  3. Schedule intake call (30-45 minutes)
    • Book on calendar with Zoom/phone link
    • Prepare interview questions document
    • Plan to gather all required information
    • Record notes during call
  4. Verify EIN with IRS database
    • Visit: https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employer-id-numbers
    • Confirm EIN matches legal business name exactly
    • Verify EIN is 9 digits in format: XX-XXXXXXX
    • Save screenshot of verification
  5. Confirm business entity registration
    • Check Secretary of State website for customer’s state
    • Verify: Business name, formation date, status (active), registered agent
    • Example for Florida: https://dos.myflorida.com/sunbiz/
    • Save copy of business entity record
    • Ensure name on FMCSA application matches EXACTLY to state filing

Phase 2: Application Preparation (Day 2-3)

WHERE TO FILE: https://li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov/LIVIEW/pkg_uis_landing.prc_login

  1. Access FMCSA Unified Registration System

    • Navigate to: https://li-public.fmcsa.dot.gov/LIVIEW/pkg_uis_landing.prc_login
    • Click “Register” (first-time users)
    • Create company account:
      • Enter EIN
      • Create username (company email recommended)
      • Create strong password (8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, symbol)
      • Enter security questions and answers
    • Verify email address (check customer’s inbox for verification link)
    • Log in to account

  2. Complete FMCSA Registration (Form MCS-150)

    Section 1: General Information

    • Legal business name (must match state registration EXACTLY)
    • Doing Business As (DBA) name if applicable
    • Physical address (no P.O. boxes)
    • Mailing address
    • Primary phone and fax (if applicable)
    • Email address
    • Select entity type: Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Sole Proprietorship
    • Enter state of formation and formation date

    Section 2: Operation Classification

    • Select “For-Hire” or “Private” carrier
    • For Interstate operations: Check “Interstate” box
    • For Intrastate only: Check applicable state
    • Most carriers select: “Interstate” for authority to cross state lines

    Section 3: Cargo Classification Select all applicable (most common):

    • General Freight (most carriers)
    • Motor Vehicles
    • Household Goods (requires additional authority)
    • Refrigerated Food
    • Logs/Poles/Beams/Lumber
    • Building Materials
    • Mobile Homes
    • Machinery (Large Objects)
    • Fresh Produce
    • Liquids/Gases
    • Intermodal Containers
    • Passengers (requires passenger authority)
    • Grain/Feed/Hay
    • Coal/Coke
    • Meat
    • Garbage/Refuse
    • US Mail
    • Chemicals
    • Commodities Dry Bulk
    • Beverages
    • Paper Products
    • Utilities
    • Farm Supplies
    • Construction
    • Water Well
    • HAZMAT (requires hazmat endorsement, additional fees)

    Section 4: Hazmat Endorsement (if applicable)

    • If transporting hazmat, check box
    • Select hazmat safety permit requirement (if applicable)
    • Additional $300 fee for hazmat authority

    Section 5: Company Officials

    • List all owners/partners/corporate officers:
      • Full legal name
      • Title (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer, Member, Partner)
      • Date of birth
      • Address
    • Must list at least one principal

    Section 6: Power Units and Drivers

    • Enter total number of power units (trucks)
    • Enter total number of drivers
    • Enter estimated annual mileage
    • These can be estimates; update later on biennial MCS-150 update

  3. Prepare Operating Authority Application (OP-1)

    Within same FMCSA portal:

    • Select “Apply for Motor Carrier Operating Authority”
    • Choose authority type:
      • Common Authority (most carriers) - haul for general public
      • Contract Authority - haul for specific shippers under contract
      • Broker Authority - if also operating as broker ($300 additional)
      • Freight Forwarder Authority ($300 additional)

    Enter operation details:

    • Confirm cargo types from MCS-150
    • Verify radius of operation (local, regional, long-haul)
    • For interstate: Select all states where you’ll operate

    Insurance Requirements:

    • General Freight: $750,000 minimum
    • Passengers (1-15): $1,500,000
    • Passengers (16+): $5,000,000
    • Hazmat: $5,000,000
    • Oil/Hazardous Substances: $1,000,000-$5,000,000

  4. Prepare BOC-3 Process Agent Designation

    What it is: Designation of agents for service of process in all states

    Option A: Use process agent service ($50-$100/year)

    • Examples: LegalRegisteredAgent.com, ProcessAgentNow.com
    • They provide agents in all 50 states
    • File BOC-3 form electronically via FMCSA portal

    Option B: Self-designate (not recommended)

    • Must designate individual agent in each state
    • Requires physical address in each state

    Filing BOC-3:

    • Within FMCSA portal, select “BOC-3”
    • Enter process agent service information
    • Upload completed BOC-3 form (provided by process agent service)
    • No additional FMCSA fee for BOC-3

  5. Prepare Unified Carrier Registration (UCR) if applicable

    • See Section 4 for detailed UCR filing steps
    • File UCR simultaneously with MC/DOT or immediately after
    • Required for interstate operations

Phase 3: Submission (Day 3-4)

  1. Review all forms with customer
    • Schedule review call or screen share
    • Go through each field to verify accuracy
    • Common errors to check:
      • Business name spelling
      • EIN format and accuracy
      • Address format (no abbreviations for USPS)
      • Phone number format
      • Cargo classifications (did we select all needed?)
    • Have customer confirm all information is correct
  2. Obtain customer signature on required documents
    • If using DocuSign or electronic signature:
      • Send via email
      • Set urgent/72-hour deadline
      • Include instructions
    • If using manual signature:
      • Email PDF for customer to print, sign, scan
      • Accept photo from phone if quality is good
    • Documents requiring signature:
      • FMCSA application (digital signature in portal)
      • BOC-3 designation (if not digital)
      • Insurance authorization forms
  3. Process FMCSA filing fee payment
    • Payment portal: Within FMCSA online registration system
    • Fee breakdown:
      • $300 for motor carrier authority (MC#)
      • $300 for DOT number (usually included)
      • $300 for hazmat endorsement (if applicable)
      • $300 for broker authority (if applicable)
    • Payment methods accepted:
      • Credit card (Visa, MasterCard, Discover, AmEx)
      • Debit card
      • ACH/Electronic check (some portals)
    • Process payment:
      • Enter payment information in portal
      • Verify billing address matches card on file
      • Click “Submit Payment”
      • Wait for confirmation screen
    • Save receipt:
      • Print to PDF or screenshot
      • Save in customer folder: Payment Receipts
      • Note confirmation number
  4. Submit application through FMCSA portal
    • Final review:
      • Check every field one last time
      • Verify attachments uploaded (insurance forms, etc.)
    • Submit:
      • Click “Submit Application” button
      • DO NOT close browser until confirmation appears
    • Confirmation:
      • You’ll receive a confirmation number (write this down immediately)
      • Application status will show “Pending”
      • USDOT number assigned immediately (write this down)
      • MC number assigned but not “active” until insurance filed
    • Email confirmation:
      • Check customer’s email for FMCSA confirmation
      • Save copy of confirmation email
      • Note: May take 24-48 hours for email confirmation
  5. Save confirmation number and submission receipt
    • Document in customer file:
      • Confirmation number: _______________
      • USDOT number: _______________
      • Pending MC number: _______________
      • Date/time submitted: _______________
      • Submitted by: _______________
    • Create tracking entry in master spreadsheet
  6. Upload all documents to customer folder
    • Organize in subfolders:
      • Applications/MCS-150.pdf
      • Applications/OP-1.pdf
      • Applications/BOC-3.pdf
      • Payment_Receipts/FMCSA_Payment_[date].pdf
      • Correspondence/Submission_Confirmation_[date].pdf
    • Update file tracking system to “Pending Insurance”

Phase 4: Insurance & Bonding (Day 4-7)

  1. Confirm insurance requirements (detailed by cargo type):

    • General Freight: $750,000 minimum liability
    • Certain Commodities (oil, hazardous substances): $1,000,000
    • Hazmat (quantities requiring placards): $5,000,000
    • Passengers (1-15): $1,500,000
    • Passengers (16+): $5,000,000
    • Note: Physical damage insurance (not required by DOT but recommended)

  2. Connect customer with insurance broker

    • Provide list of commercial truck insurance brokers
    • Recommended: Brokers familiar with FMCSA requirements
    • Customer must obtain:
      • Commercial auto liability policy
      • General liability policy
      • Cargo insurance (optional but recommended)
      • Physical damage coverage (optional)

  3. Ensure insurance provider files BMC-91 or BMC-91X electronically

    What it is: Form insurance company files with FMCSA to guarantee coverage

    Two types:

    • Form BMC-91: Surety bond (less common)
    • Form BMC-91X: Insurance policy endorsement (most common)

    Filing process (done by insurance company):

    • Insurance company files directly with FMCSA electronically
    • Must include:
      • Carrier’s USDOT number
      • Carrier’s pending MC number
      • Policy number
      • Coverage amounts
      • Effective date
    • FMCSA system confirms receipt (usually within 24 hours)

    Your responsibilities:

    • Confirm insurance broker WILL file BMC-91X with FMCSA
    • Obtain copy of insurance certificate from broker
    • Verify certificate shows:
      • USDOT number
      • MC number
      • Coverage amounts meeting minimum requirements
      • Named insured matches exactly to FMCSA application
    • Upload certificate to customer folder: Insurance/Certificate_[date].pdf

  4. Monitor for insurance filing confirmation from FMCSA

    How to monitor:

    • Method 1: Check FMCSA SAFER system (see Phase 5)
      • Look for “Insurance on File” status
      • Will show: Insurance carrier name, policy number, coverage amount, effective date
    • Method 2: Log in to FMCSA online portal
      • Check application status
      • Look for insurance filing confirmation
      • Download confirmation if available
    • Method 3: Call FMCSA at 1-800-832-5660
      • Provide USDOT number
      • Ask: “Has insurance been filed for this carrier?”

    Timeline:

    • Insurance company typically files within 1-3 business days
    • FMCSA processes within 24 hours of filing
    • If not filed after 5 days, contact insurance broker

  5. Arrange bond or trust (BMC-84/BMC-85) if needed

    When required:

    • Freight brokers: $75,000 bond (BMC-84 or BMC-85)
    • Freight forwarders: $75,000 bond
    • Motor carriers: Generally NOT required (use insurance instead)

    If needed:

    • Contact surety bond provider (insurance broker can arrange)
    • Forms:
      • BMC-84: Surety bond (most common)
      • BMC-85: Trust fund agreement
    • Cost: Typically $1,500-$3,000 annually for $75,000 bond
    • Bond company files directly with FMCSA

Phase 5: Monitoring & Activation (Day 7-21)

  1. Check FMCSA SAFER system daily for status updates

    How to access SAFER:

    • Navigate to: https://safer.fmcsa.dot.gov
    • Click “Company Snapshot”
    • Enter USDOT number or MC number
    • Review status indicators:

    Status indicators to monitor:

    • Entity Type: Should show “Carrier”
    • Operating Status: Watch for change from “Not Authorized” to “Authorized For Property”
    • Out of Service: Must show “No”
    • Insurance on File: Must show insurance carrier name and coverage amount
    • Insurance Required: Shows minimum required
    • Latest Update: Check date to see if anything changed

    Daily monitoring routine:

    • Set calendar reminder: Check SAFER at 9:00 AM daily
    • Document in customer notes: “Checked SAFER [date] - Status: [status]”
    • Look for changes in:
      • Operating Status
      • Insurance status
      • Any deficiency notices

  2. Monitor email for FMCSA correspondence

    • Check customer’s email inbox daily
    • Look for emails from: @dot.gov
    • Common subject lines:
      • “FMCSA Application Status Update”
      • “Insurance Filed”
      • “Deficiency Letter”
      • “Operating Authority Granted”
    • Save all emails to customer folder: Correspondence/
    • Forward to yourself for tracking

  3. Address any deficiency letters immediately (same day)

    Common deficiencies:

    • Insurance issue: Coverage amount insufficient, wrong policy type
      • Call insurance broker immediately
      • Request corrected BMC-91X filing
      • Deadline: Usually 30 days to correct
    • Application error: Incorrect information, missing field
      • Log in to FMCSA portal
      • Select “Amend Application”
      • Correct error and resubmit
      • No additional fee if within 30 days
    • BOC-3 missing or incomplete:
      • Verify process agent service filed BOC-3
      • Re-file if necessary

    Response protocol:

    • Read deficiency letter carefully
    • Identify exact issue
    • Develop action plan (same day)
    • Contact customer to explain
    • Take corrective action within 24-48 hours
    • Document response in customer file
    • Follow up to confirm deficiency cleared

  4. Once authority granted:

    When this happens:

    • SAFER system shows “Authorized For Property”
    • Operating Status changes from “Not Authorized” to “Authorized”
    • Usually 7-21 days after insurance filed
    • FMCSA sends confirmation email

    Your action steps:

    A. Download and save operating authority letter

    • Log in to FMCSA portal
    • Navigate to “Documents”
    • Download “Operating Authority Grant Letter” PDF
    • Save to customer folder: Completed/Authority_Grant_Letter_[date].pdf
    • Print copy for customer packet

    B. Create SAFER System snapshot

    • Visit https://safer.fmcsa.dot.gov
    • Enter USDOT number
    • Print to PDF: “Company Snapshot”
    • Shows active authority and insurance
    • Save to customer folder: Completed/SAFER_Snapshot_[date].pdf

    C. Prepare customer congratulations packet:

    • Cover letter congratulating them
    • Operating Authority Grant Letter
    • SAFER Snapshot showing active status
    • MC number documentation (your MC #is: MC-XXXXXX)
    • DOT number documentation (your DOT # is: XXXXXX)
    • Insurance certificate copy
    • Instructions for:
      • Displaying DOT number on trucks (2” letters, both sides, contrasting color)
      • Keeping documents in vehicles
      • Next steps: UCR, 2290, IFTA/IRP
    • Checklist of ongoing compliance requirements

    D. Send packet to customer

    • Email PDF packet immediately
    • Mail physical packet via USPS
    • Call customer to celebrate and explain
    • Verify they understand what to do next

    E. Provide MC# and DOT# in usable formats

    • Email format:

      Congratulations! Your operating authority is ACTIVE.

Your MC Number: MC-123456
Your DOT Number: 1234567

These numbers must be displayed on both sides of your commercial vehicles
in letters at least 2 inches tall in a contrasting color.

    • Provide files:

      • Printable door decal template (PDF)
      • Text file with numbers for decal companies

  5. Update internal tracking system to “COMPLETE”

    • Master spreadsheet: Change status from “Pending” to “Complete”
    • Add completion date
    • Add MC number and DOT number to record
    • Set follow-up reminder for 30 days post-activation
    • Move customer file to Completed/2026/ABC_Trucking_LLC/
    • Create note for future reference

File Tracking

  • Folder Structure: Customers/Jayboi Services/MC-DOT/[Year]/[Customer Name]
  • Documents to Maintain:
    • Initial application forms (MCS-150, OP-1)
    • BOC-3 designation
    • Insurance certificates
    • Authority grant letter
    • Payment receipts
    • Email correspondence log

Customer Satisfaction Checkpoints

  • ✅ Respond to customer within 4 hours
  • ✅ Provide weekly status updates
  • ✅ Explain each step in plain language
  • ✅ Set realistic timeline expectations
  • ✅ Follow up 30 days post-activation for additional needs

2. Driver Qualification File (DQF)

Service Fee: $223.00/year (Jayboi Services) or $40/month Non-CDL, $100/month Bundle (Jayboi)
Timeline: 3-5 business days for initial setup
Objective: Maintain FMCSA-compliant driver files

Required Documentation from Customer

Step-by-Step Process

Phase 1: New Driver File Creation (Day 1)

  1. Create digital driver folder: [Driver Name] - DQF - [Hire Date]
  2. Send driver information packet via email
  3. Provide driver with required forms:
    • Employment application (§391.21)
    • Inquiry to previous employers (§391.23)
    • Driver certification (§391.31)
    • Medical certification (§391.43)

Phase 2: Document Collection (Day 1-3)

  1. Employment Application
    • Review for completeness
    • Verify 10-year employment history
    • Check for gaps in employment
    • Flag any accidents or violations
  2. Motor Vehicle Record (MVR)
    • Order MVR from driver’s state of licensure
    • Review for disqualifying violations
    • Check for suspended/revoked licenses
    • Document all traffic violations
  3. Previous Employer Verification
    • Send §391.23 inquiry letters to all employers (last 3 years)
    • Allow 30 days for response
    • Document non-responses and follow up
    • Review for alcohol/drug violations, accidents, or terminations
  4. Road Test or Certificate
    • If new to CMV: Schedule road test with qualified examiner
    • If experienced: Obtain certificate from previous employer
    • File completed road test certificate

Phase 3: Medical & Drug Testing (Day 2-4)

  1. DOT Physical Examination
    • Verify medical examiner is FMCSA-certified
    • Check National Registry of Certified Medical Examiners
    • Ensure certificate is current (max 2 years)
    • Upload copy to driver file
  2. Pre-Employment Drug Test
    • Schedule test with certified collection site
    • Verify 5-panel DOT drug screen
    • Await negative result before driver operates CMV
    • File results in DQF

Phase 4: Annual Maintenance

  1. Set calendar reminders for:
    • Annual MVR review (every 12 months)
    • Medical certificate expiration
    • Annual driver certification review
  2. Update file with:
    • New violations or accidents
    • Annual inquiry to driving record
    • Updated medical certificates
  3. Document annual review with signature and date

File Tracking

  • Folder Structure: Customers/DQF/[Customer Name]/[Driver Name]
  • Required Documents Checklist:
    • ✅ Employment application (§391.21)
    • ✅ MVR (§391.23(a)(2))
    • ✅ Previous employer inquiries (§391.23)
    • ✅ Road test certificate (§391.31)
    • ✅ Medical examiner’s certificate (§391.43)
    • ✅ Pre-employment drug test (§382.301)
    • ✅ Annual review (§391.25)
    • ✅ Annual MVR (§391.25(a))

Customer Satisfaction Checkpoints

  • ✅ Explain DQF requirements in onboarding call
  • ✅ Provide driver-friendly checklist
  • ✅ Send automated renewal reminders 60 days before expiration
  • ✅ Make files available for roadside inspections
  • ✅ Conduct quarterly compliance reviews with customer

3. Drug & Alcohol Consortium

Service Fee: $100.00/year (Jayboi Services) or included in bundles (Jayboi)
Timeline: Immediate enrollment
Objective: Compliance with DOT drug and alcohol testing requirements

Required Documentation from Customer

Step-by-Step Process

Phase 1: Enrollment (Day 1)

  1. Create consortium account for customer
    • Enter company information
    • Assign unique account ID
    • Set up DER login credentials
  2. Add drivers to random pool
    • Input driver information accurately
    • Assign driver IDs
    • Set testing pools (DOT-regulated only)
  3. Send welcome packet to customer with:
    • DER login information
    • Testing guidelines
    • Collection site locations
    • Emergency contact numbers

Phase 2: Random Selection & Testing

  1. Random Selection Process:
    • Use FMCSA-approved random selection software
    • Maintain 50% annual rate for drugs, 10% for alcohol
    • Generate monthly/quarterly random selections
    • Ensure truly random, unbiased selections
  2. Notification:
    • Notify DER of selected drivers immediately
    • Send driver notification letter/email
    • Testing must occur within designated timeframe
    • Document notification date and time
  3. Collection:
    • Direct driver to nearest collection site
    • Provide collection site with CCF (chain of custody form)
    • Ensure proper specimen collection procedures
    • Verify collector is DOT-certified

Phase 3: Other Testing Types

  1. Pre-Employment Testing:
    • Schedule upon customer request
    • Verify negative result before driver operates
    • File results in DQF
  2. Post-Accident Testing:
    • Must occur within 8 hours for alcohol, 32 hours for drugs
    • Required for: Fatality, citation following accident
    • Provide immediate testing authorization
  3. Reasonable Suspicion Testing:
    • Based on trained supervisor observation
    • Document specific, contemporaneous behaviors
    • Test immediately for alcohol, as soon as possible for drugs
  4. Return-to-Duty & Follow-Up:
    • After violation and SAP completion
    • Minimum 6 follow-up tests over 12 months
    • Coordinate with Substance Abuse Professional (SAP)

Phase 4: Results Management & Reporting

  1. Receive test results from MRO (Medical Review Officer)
  2. Notify DER within 24 hours
  3. For positive results:
    • Inform DER of immediate removal from safety-sensitive duties
    • Provide SAP referral information
    • Document violation in clearinghouse
  4. Maintain 5-year record retention
  5. Submit annual FMCSA summary reports

File Tracking

  • Folder Structure: Customers/Consortium/[Customer Name]/[Year]
  • Documents to Maintain:
    • Enrollment forms
    • DER authorization
    • Random selection records
    • Test authorization forms
    • Lab results (negative only, MRO maintains positives)
    • Clearinghouse registration
    • Annual summary reports

Customer Satisfaction Checkpoints

  • ✅ Provide DER with 24/7 emergency testing hotline
  • ✅ Send monthly testing compliance reports
  • ✅ Offer DER training on regulations
  • ✅ Respond to testing requests within 30 minutes
  • ✅ Maintain network of convenient collection sites

4. UCR Annual Registration

Service Fee: Starts at $170.00 (varies by fleet size)
Timeline: Same-day processing
Objective: Register with Unified Carrier Registration system
Filing Portal: https://www.ucr.gov (redirects to state-specific portals)

Required Documentation from Customer

Understanding UCR

What is UCR?

  • Unified Carrier Registration replaced the Single State Registration System (SSRS)
  • Required for interstate motor carriers, brokers, freight forwarders, and leasing companies
  • Registration year runs January 1 - December 31
  • Based on fleet size, not revenue

Who needs UCR?

  • Any carrier operating commercial vehicles in interstate commerce
  • Brokers and freight forwarders
  • Leasing companies of commercial vehicles

Exemptions:

  • Intrastate-only carriers (stay within one state)
  • Government-owned vehicles
  • Recreational vehicles

Step-by-Step Process

Phase 1: Registration Preparation (Same Day)

  1. Verify customer’s MC/DOT number in SAFER

    • Visit: https://safer.fmcsa.dot.gov
    • Enter USDOT number or MC number
    • Verify: Legal name, address, operating status
    • Confirm interstate authority (if intrastate only, UCR not required)
    • Screenshot for your records

  2. Determine UCR fleet size bracket

    2026 UCR Fee Structure:

    • 0-2 vehicles: $76 per year
    • 3-5 vehicles: $230 per year
    • 6-20 vehicles: $691 per year
    • 21-100 vehicles: $3,459 per year
    • 101-1,000 vehicles: $20,955 per year
    • 1,001-2,000 vehicles: $64,165 per year
    • 2,001-5,000 vehicles: $67,624 per year
    • 5,001-10,000 vehicles: $71,084 per year
    • 10,001+ vehicles: $74,543 per year

    Important: Ask customer for TOTAL fleet count, including:

    • Owned trucks
    • Leased trucks
    • Trailers (some states count trailers)
    • All commercial vehicles in operation

    Verify bracket:

    • Count carefully - wrong bracket = wrong fee = rejected application
    • If on the edge (e.g., 5 vs 6 vehicles), ask customer to confirm exact count
    • Document fleet count in customer file

  3. Calculate correct filing fee based on bracket

    • Your service fee + UCR government fee
    • Example: 3 vehicles = $230 UCR fee + $170 service fee = $400 total to customer
    • Prepare invoice for customer

  4. Confirm state of base registration

    • This is where vehicles are registered (license plates)
    • Usually customer’s home state
    • Determines which state UCR portal to use
    • Each state has its own online filing system

Phase 2: Filing (Same Day)

STATE-SPECIFIC UCR PORTALS:

Each state has its own portal. Access via: https://www.ucr.gov/getting_started/state_contacts.php

Common State Portals:

  • California: https://www.california-ucr.org
  • Texas: https://www.texas-ucr.org
  • Florida: https://www.flhsmv.gov/ucr
  • Ohio: https://ohioucr.com
  • Indiana: https://indiana-ucr.org
  • Illinois: https://illinois-ucr.org
  • Pennsylvania: https://pennsylvania-ucr.org
  • Georgia: https://georgia-ucr.org
  • North Carolina: https://northcarolina-ucr.org
  • Michigan: https://michigan-ucr.org

General Filing Process (varies slightly by state):

  1. Access your state’s UCR online registration portal
    • Find your state at: https://www.ucr.gov
    • Click on your state from the list
    • Click “Register” or “Create Account” (first-time filers)
  2. Create account (if first time)
    • Enter USDOT number
    • Enter business information

Understanding Form 2290

What is Form 2290?

  • Heavy Vehicle Use Tax (HVUT) - annual federal tax
  • Required for vehicles 55,000 lbs or more gross weight
  • Tax year runs July 1 - June 30
  • Must file by last day of month following first use month

When to file:

  • Annual Filing: By August 31 for vehicles used in July
  • First Use: By last day of month following first use
  • Weight Increase: When increasing to higher tax bracket
  • VIN Correction: Anytime to correct prior filing error

Who must file:

  • Owner or operator of highway motor vehicle 55,000+ lbs
  • Vehicle registered or required to be registered in owner’s name
  • Includes trucks, truck tractors, b uses

Exemptions:

  • Vehicles under 55,000 lbs
  • Suspended vehicles (less than 5,000 miles/7,500 for agricultural)
  • Government vehicles
  • Certain non-highway vehicles

2290 Tax Tables (2026 Tax Year)

Tax Due by Weight Category:

Taxable Gross Weight Annual Tax Prorated (per month remaining)
55,000 lbs $100 ~$8.33/month
56,000 - 57,000 lbs $222 ~$18.50/month
58,000 - 59,000 lbs $312 ~$26/month
60,000 - 62,000 lbs $550 ~$45.83/month
63,000 - 65,000 lbs $600 ~$50/month
66,000 - 68,000 lbs $650 ~$54.17/month
69,000 - 73,000 lbs $700 ~$58.33/month
74,000 - 76,000 lbs $750 ~$62.50/month
77,000 - 80,000 lbs $800 ~$66.67/month

Proration Formula:

  • Tax due = (Annual tax ÷ 12) × Number of months remaining in tax year
  • Tax year: July - June
  • Example: First use in January = 6 months remaining = 50% of annual tax

Step-by-Step Process

Phase 1: Information Gathering (Day 1)

  1. Create 2290 filing folder:

  2. Collect vehicle information (for each vehicle):

    A. VIN (Vehicle Identification Number):

    • Must be exactly 17 characters
    • No spaces, letters/numbers only
    • Common errors to avoid:
      • Letter “O” vs number “0”
      • Letter “I” vs number “1”
      • Leaving out characters
    • How to verify VIN:
      • Check vehicle registration
      • Look at driver’s side dashboard (visible through windshield)
      • Check driver’s door jamb sticker
      • Verify with customer: Read VIN aloud, spell it back
    • Document: VIN: 1HGBH41JXMN109186 (example)

    B. Taxable Gross Weight:

    • This is the TOTAL weight the vehicle is registered to carry
    • Includes: Vehicle + cargo + fuel + passengers
    • Where to find:
      • Vehicle registration (GVWR or GVW)
      • Manufacturer’s weight rating
      • State DMV registration
    • Round UP to higher tax bracket if unsure
    • Document: Gross Weight: 80,000 lbs

    C. First Use Month:

    • Month vehicle was FIRST used on public highways at taxable weight
    • For annual renewal: July (most common)
    • For new vehicle: Month purchased and first driven
    • For weight increase: Month weight increased
    • Critical: Must file by last day of month FOLLOWING first use
      • First use in July → File by August 31
      • First use in January → File by February 28/29
    • Document: First Use: July 2025

  3. Determine filing category:

    Category A: New Vehicle First Use

    • Brand new vehicle being registered
    • File when: First used on highway
    • Deadline: Last day of month following first use
    • Tax: Prorated from first use month through June

    Category B: Annual Renewal

    • Vehicle was used in prior tax year
    • First use month: July
    • Deadline: August 31
    • Tax: Full annual amount (July-June)

    Category C: Increase in Taxable Gross Weight

    • Vehicle weight rating increased during tax year
    • Moved to higher tax bracket
    • File supplemental return
    • Tax: Difference between old and new tax bracket, prorated

    Category D: VIN Correction

    • Error on previously filed 2290
    • Wrong VIN entered
    • No additional tax due (usually)
    • Need original Schedule 1 for reference

Phase 2: Tax Calculation & Preparation (Day 1)

  1. Calculate tax due for each vehicle:

    For Annual Renewal (July first use):

    • Tax = Full annual amount from tax table
    • Example: 80,000 lbs = $800

    For Mid-Year First Use:

    • Determine months remaining in tax year (through June)
    • Tax = (Annual tax ÷ 12) × months remaining
    • Example: First use January, 80,000 lbs:
      • Months remaining: Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun = 6 months
      • Tax = ($800 ÷ 12) × 6 = $400

    For Weight Increase:

    • Find tax for OLD weight bracket
    • Find tax for NEW weight bracket
    • Difference = New - Old
    • Prorate difference for months remaining
    • Example: Increased from 65,000 to 80,000 in March:
      • Old bracket: $600/year
      • New bracket: $800/year
      • Difference: $200
      • Months remaining (Mar-Jun): 4 months
      • Additional tax: ($200 ÷ 12) × 4 = $66.67

    For Suspended Vehicle:

    • Logging under 5,000 miles (7,500 for agricultural)
    • Tax: $0 (but must file to claim suspension)

  • 2026-01-10 - XYZ Logistics - DQF John Smith - Complete

Status Tracking

Status Categories:

  • Initiated: Customer contacted, information gathering
  • In Progress: Actively working on service
  • Pending Customer: Awaiting customer documentation/payment
  • Pending Authority: Awaiting FMCSA, IRS, or third-party response
  • Complete: Service delivered, filed, and confirmed
  • Recurring: Ongoing service requiring regular updates

Document Retention

Retention Periods (Minimum):

  • MC/DOT applications: Permanent
  • Driver Qualification Files: 3 years after driver termination
  • Drug & alcohol testing records: 5 years
  • UCR registrations: 3 years
  • 2290 filings: 3 years (per IRS requirements)
  • Dispatch records: 6 months (freight bills/BOLs)
  • Invoices and payment records: 7 years

Backup Procedures:

  1. Daily auto-backup to cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive)
  2. Weekly external hard drive backup
  3. Monthly archive of completed files
  4. Annual audit of retention compliance

Handling Issues & Complaints

Response Protocol:

  1. Acknowledge immediately (within 1 hour during business hours)
    • “Thank you for bringing this to my attention. I’m looking into it right now.”
  2. Investigate thoroughly
    • Review all documentation
    • Identify root cause
    • Determine resolution options
  3. Respond with solution (within 4 hours)
    • Explain what happened
    • Outline how you’ll fix it
    • Provide timeline
    • Offer compensation if appropriate (fee reduction, expedited service)
  4. Follow up after resolution
    • Confirm customer satisfaction
    • Document lesson learned
    • Update procedures if needed

Escalation Path:

  • Frontline staff → Service Manager → Owner/Director
