FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common Questions

What is the IRS Form 2290?

Form 2290 is the Heavy Highway Vehicle Use Tax Return. The IRS requires anyone registering a heavy highway motor vehicle with a taxable gross weight of 55,000 pounds or more to file Form 2290 and pay the tax.

When is the Form 2290 due?

The annual Form 2290 filing season runs from July 1st through August 31st each year. If you buy a new truck, your 2290 is due by the last day of the month following the month the vehicle was first used on public highways.

What happens if I don't file my 2290 on time?

The IRS penalty for filing your Form 2290 late is 4.5% of the total tax due, assessed monthly for up to five months. You will also face a late payment penalty of 0.5% of the total tax due, plus additional interest charges.

How fast will I get my Stamped Schedule 1?

When you use Jayboi Services to e-file your Form 2290, we guarantee you will receive your official IRS-stamped Schedule 1 within 2 hours of payment, allowing you to get your tags or renew your registration immediately.
